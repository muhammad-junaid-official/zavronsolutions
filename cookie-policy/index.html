Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026. Explanation of cookies and analytics technologies.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work properly, enhance user navigation, and report site traffic metrics.

2. Cookies We Use

We use essential technical cookies to enable seamless navigation and form wizard state persistence, as well as aggregated analytical cookies (e.g. Google Analytics) to monitor website performance and Core Web Vitals.

3. Controlling Cookies

You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies. Note that certain interactive wizard features may require essential cookies to maintain form progress.
