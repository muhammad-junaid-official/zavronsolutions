ABOUT ZAVRON SOLUTIONS

A Full-Service Web Development & SEO Agency in the USA

Zavron Solutions is a US web development and SEO agency that designs, builds and ranks business websites. We combine custom web development, WordPress and e-commerce engineering with technical SEO, local SEO, paid media and AI search optimization, so one senior team owns your website from first wireframe to first-page Google rankings.

Most businesses hire one company to build a website and another to market it, and the two rarely agree on speed, structure or keywords. We remove that gap. Every website we develop ships with clean semantic HTML, Core Web Vitals scores in the green, Schema.org structured data and a keyword map built around the searches your buyers actually make. That foundation is what lets our SEO services compound month after month instead of fighting the code underneath them.

Our clients are small businesses, local service companies, healthcare practices, law firms, real estate teams, e-commerce brands and B2B SaaS companies across all 50 states. Whether you need a new custom website, a faster WordPress site, a Shopify or WooCommerce store, or an SEO agency that can lift rankings for an existing site, Zavron Solutions delivers measurable growth in organic traffic, qualified leads and revenue.

  • Custom web development — fast, secure, mobile-first websites and web apps with no template bloat.
  • SEO services — technical SEO, on-page SEO, local SEO and content that ranks on Google's first page.
  • AI search optimization (GEO) — structured data, llms.txt and answer-first content so ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can cite you.
  • E-commerce and digital marketing — conversion-focused stores, Google Ads and social campaigns that grow revenue.
